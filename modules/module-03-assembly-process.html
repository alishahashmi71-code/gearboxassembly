From Specification to In-Service

A disciplined three-stage assembly process — every time, no shortcuts.

1

Specify & Source

We review your application requirements — speed, torque, mounting configuration, and operating environment — then source every component before a single bolt is turned. Our application engineers assist with OEM interchange, obsolete part replacement, and gearing selection.

  • Application requirements analysis
  • OEM cross-referencing and obsolete part sourcing
  • Component verification before build begins
 
2

Build & Test

Experienced technicians assemble the gearbox from new components using correct torque, alignment, and clearance specifications throughout. After assembly, the completed unit is tested to verify it meets your required performance specs before it leaves the shop.

  • Precision assembly by experienced technicians
  • Torque, alignment, and clearance verification
  • Performance testing before shipment
